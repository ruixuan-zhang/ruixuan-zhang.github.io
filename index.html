Ruixuan Zhang

Postdoctoral Researcher · Kyoto University

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Ogata Lab (Microbial Ecology and Evolution) at Kyoto University. My primary expertise lies in bioinformatics and computational simulation. I integrate bioinformatics with experimental validation to understand how genomes evolve.

I earned my Ph.D. at Kyoto University under the supervision of Prof. Hiroyuki Ogata, where I studied the mechanisms by which giant viruses hijack host translation systems. I conducted multi-omics experiments—including RNA-seq, Ribo-seq, and tRNA-seq—with Prof. Shintaro Iwasaki at RIKEN, and performed in situ fluorescent labeling with Assist. Prof. Anouk Willemsen at the University of Vienna. I also isolated giant viruses with Prof. Masaharu Takemura at Tokyo University of Science and Dr. Hiroyuki Hikida at NIID.

Currently, I am developing a novel method for CAZyme annotation with Prof. Yanbin Yin at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln.

My work has been kindly supported by JSPS DC2 Fellowship, a Sasakawa Scientific Research Grant, and a JST Fellowship in Kyoto University. Here are my publication list, curriculum vitae, and my Research interest.

Beyond science, I am a naturalist who enjoys hiking and strength training.
